# ATOMS ability taxonomy (ToMBench)

ToMBench organizes its items under the ATOMS framework (Abilities in Theory of Mind Space):
6 higher-level categories comprising 31 fine-grained abilities. This taxonomy is the basis for
the ability-stratified train/validation/test split (Section 3.2 of the manuscript) and for the
category- and ability-level result tables (Sections 4.3–4.4).
